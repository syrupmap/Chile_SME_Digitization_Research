# Chile_SME_Digitization_Research

R-based policy analysis of Chilean SME digitalization programs using government administrative data and municipal poverty indicators. This project was completed as part of the Undergraduate Research Apprenticeship Program (URAP) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
Mentor: Adrian Wong

## Overview 
This analysis investigates the geographic distribution of digitalization services provided to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) across Chile. Data was cleaned, categorized, and tagged with poverty indicators, regions, and sectioning to explore how digital support programs were distributed among communities with varying socioeconomic conditions.

## Research Objectives
- Clean and standardize administrative government datasets
- Analyze the regional distribution of SME digitalization services
- Tie in with Literature Review
- Explore whether higher-poverty municipalities received comparable levels of support
- Produce visualizations for policy and research discussions

## Methods
The analysis was conducted in R. 
- dplyr for data transformation
- stringr and stringi for text normalization
- readxl for data ingestion
- ggplot2 for visualization

## Technologies
- R
- dplyr
- ggplot
- readxl
- stringr, stringi

## Data Availability
The original datasets used in this research are not included in this repository. They were provided through a research collaboration and may contain restricted or non-public information. To demonstrate the methodology while preserving data privacy, this repository uses placeholder's.
